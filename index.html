    About me

    I lead the physics technology group in the Department of Radiation Oncology at Shuguang Hospital, Shanghai University of Traditional Chinese Medicine. I trained in medical physics at Fudan University (M.S.), and I sit on the Physics Group of the Branch Association of Radiation Oncology of Shanghai Medical Association and on the committee of the Medical Imaging Computing Seminar (MICS). Along the way I have led four Shanghai municipal and other grants and filed seven patent applications, two of them granted.
